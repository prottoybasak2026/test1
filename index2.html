Cape Verde

Cape Verde Cape Verde, officially known as Cabo Verde, is an island country in the Atlantic Ocean off the west coast of Africa. It consists of ten volcanic islands and has a population of about 600,000 people. The capital city is Praia. Portuguese is the official language, while Cape Verdean Creole is widely spoken. The country is famous for its beautiful beaches, pleasant climate, music, and tourism. Fishing, tourism, and services are important parts of its economy. Cape Verde is known as one of the most peaceful and stable countries in Africa.
