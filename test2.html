Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a beautiful country in South Asia. It became independent in 1971. The capital city is Dhaka. The official language is Bengali (Bangla), and the currency is the Bangladeshi Taka. Bangladesh is known for itsl green fields, many rivers, and the world's largest mangrove forest, the Sundarbans. Agriculture, the garment industry, and remittances are important parts of the country's economy. The people of Bangladesh are friendly and hardworking. Bangladesh is making progress in education, technology, and economic development.
